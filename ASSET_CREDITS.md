# Artwork and implementation

The city is drawn from original SVG geometry in `scene.js`. Architecture, terrain, roads, props, people, Pepper, vehicles, the fountain, basketball court and Ferris wheel are separate scene objects. There is no generated city background, photo, external sprite pack, or remote image.

The supplied isometric city reference informed the visual direction. Its artwork is not copied or redistributed. Montréal landmarks are stylized narrative anchors, not a surveyed map or exact workplace locations.

`assets/favicon.svg` is the existing project icon. The original supplied résumé PDFs remain preserved in the repository but are not linked by the interface.
